# Roadmap Traceability Matrix

---

## 1. Traceability Methodology

This matrix establishes an unbroken, bidirectional audit trail connecting every section, checklist item, command, principle, and completion criterion in `java-backend-engineering-prerequisites-roadmap.md` to its designated canonical concept module.

### Mapping Classification
- **Direct 1:1:** A source checklist item maps directly to a dedicated concept module.
- **Split 1:Many:** A broad or compound source item is split across multiple focused concept modules for independent mastery.
- **Synthesized Many:1:** Closely related syntactic or utility items grouped into a cohesive concept module to prevent pedagogical over-splitting.
- **Audit Addition:** An essential engineering topic discovered during the missing topics audit and added to bridge a prerequisite gap.
